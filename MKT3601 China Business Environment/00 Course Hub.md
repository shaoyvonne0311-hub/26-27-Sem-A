# MKT3601 China Business Environment - Course Hub
- **Exam:** Tue 15 Dec, 09:30-12:30 window, **2 hours of writing**. 40% of grade. **Open book** (all teaching materials, no page limit, no laptop/iPad/phone).
- **Format:** 3 essay questions, answer 2. **Q1 compulsory** = key concepts, definitions/meaning from every week. Then choose 1 of 2 discussion-style essays (like group-discussion questions, e.g. SEO and private company). About 1 hour per question.
- **Marking:** width/depth, clear structure, logical conclusion; apply to new situations, analyse relationships, evaluate with evidence. Own opinion + data/events/real examples. Concepts and theory are the essential part.
- **Not in exam:** corruption.

| 10 | Sustainable development | Scope deck + teaching slides (second deck) |
## Approach
1. Know the starred concepts by heart (Q1) - a glossary page per week.
2. Predict the 2 long questions from the Week 10 scope + each week's classwork + group-discussion questions.
3. Practise handwriting 1-hour essays (speed) with a strong own opinion and solid support (data, events, real-world examples).
4. Build and tab the open-book binder (it makes standing out harder, so prep matters).
