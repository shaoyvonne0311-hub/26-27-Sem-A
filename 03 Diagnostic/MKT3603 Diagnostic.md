# MKT3603 诊断卷 Diagnostic（40 min / 42 marks）

> [!warning] 无 pp 的替代
> MKT3603 没有 past paper，这卷按 in-class quiz 的题型（场景 + which concept）自拟，难度按 quiz 4/5 的水平。

## Part A — 15 × 2 = 30 marks

Choose the concept each scenario **best** illustrates.

**1.** A consumer buys a ¥60,000 watch mainly because it signals success to peers.  
A. need for cognition　B. conspicuous consumption　C. utilitarian value　D. habituation

**2.** After paying ¥2,000 for a concert ticket, Maya stays though she is bored, reasoning “I already paid”.  
A. sunk cost effect　B. endowment effect　C. anchoring　D. framing

**3.** While shopping for a car, Leo suddenly notices every car ad he previously ignored.  
A. perceptual vigilance　B. mere exposure　C. selective retention　D. Weber’s law

**4.** A bag priced ¥9,999 is marked down to ¥6,999 and feels like a bargain.  
A. mental accounting　B. price–quality inference　C. anchoring　D. scarcity

**5.** Priya keeps buying the same detergent “because it always works”, without comparing alternatives.  
A. attitude strength　B. brand loyalty　C. variety seeking　D. impulse buying

**6.** A traveller recalls only the excellent service at a hotel and forgets the noisy room.  
A. selective retention　B. primacy effect　C. flashbulb memory　D. nostalgia

**7.** Given three subscriptions, most consumers pick the middle one.  
A. compromise effect　B. decoy effect　C. default bias　D. loss aversion

**8.** A teenager buys a specific sneaker to be accepted by her friend group.  
A. informational influence　B. normative influence　C. self-concept　D. attribution

**9.** “My car says who I am” best reflects  
A. extended self　B. ideal self　C. social comparison　D. self-monitoring

**10.** A sedentary office worker buys a gym membership to feel like a “healthy person”.  
A. approach–avoidance conflict　B. ideal self　C. hedonic motive　D. cognitive dissonance

**11.** After repeated free samples, a shopper starts to like a new tea brand.  
A. mere exposure　B. classical conditioning　C. social proof　D. anchoring

**12.** A ¥3 price rise is noticed on a ¥20 item but ignored on a ¥20,000 watch.  
A. absolute threshold　B. just noticeable difference　C. sensory adaptation　D. contrast effect

**13.** A supermarket places milk at the back so shoppers pass other aisles.  
A. store atmosphere　B. shelf positioning　C. retail theatre　D. assortment depth

**14.** People remember the first and last brands in an ad sequence better than the middle ones.  
A. primacy and recency　B. rehearsal　C. chunking　D. spacing effect

**15.** A student wants a new laptop but worries about draining savings.  
A. approach–approach conflict　B. avoidance–avoidance conflict　C. approach–avoidance conflict　D. cognitive conflict

## Part B — 3 × 4 = 12 marks

**B1.** Explain the sunk cost effect and mental accounting. Give one purchase example and say why consumers violate rationality. **(JUDGE)**

**B2.** Contrast attitude with memory. How does a strong attitude change choice behaviour? **(ATT)**

**B3.** How does a reference group influence purchase? Use brand community as an example. **(SOCIAL)**
