# MKT3601 诊断卷 Diagnostic（50 min / 50 marks）

> [!warning] 闭卷写
> 正式考试开卷只准纸质；但诊断要闭卷，才看得出你是「真会」还是「会翻」。答案用英文写，术语英文、解释可中英混排。

## Q1 Weekly concepts（6 × 2 = 12 marks）

Explain each in 1–2 sentences, with one China example:
1. Consumption upgrading vs downgrading **(TRENDS)**
2. Tier-1 / tier-3 city gap **(REGION)**
3. Planned economy to market transition **(ECON-TRANS)**
4. KOL vs KOC **(KOL)**
5. Social e-commerce (拼单/直播) **(ECOMM)**
6. Guanxi and its business role **(GUANXI)**

## Q2 Luxury case（20 marks）

Maison Lumière, a French heritage leather house, plans to enter China in 2027 with 5 boutiques and a Tmall flagship.

(a) Which consumer segment would you target, and how would you define it? **(SEG, 4)**
(b) What entry mode would you choose and what are the two biggest entry issues? **(ENTRY-ISSUES, 4)**
(c) Give three de-risking moves (pricing, channel, or brand). **(DERISK, 4)**
(d) How should it balance global luxury standards with local taste? **(LUX-STD, 4)**
(e) What role should KOL / social commerce play, and what is the risk? **(KOL + ECOMM, 4)**

## Q3 Essay（18 marks）

“China’s SOE reform and the sustainability agenda are two sides of the same governance problem.” Discuss with examples, and link to consumer-market outcomes. **(SOE + SUSTAIN)**

1. 对答案看关键词命中（不是看字数）。
2. 填 Excel `07 诊断 Diagnostic`；case 五问按小问分别计分。

> [!success]- 答案（做完再看）
> Q1 每题踩两个关键词即可：升级/降级、一线/三线、计划→市场、KOL vs KOC、直播/拼单、关系网络。
> Q2 五小问各答 2 点：segment 维度、entry mode+issue、3 条 derisk、全球 vs 本地、KOL 角色+风险。
> Q3 需要 SOE 改革三点 + ESG/绿色消费 + 共同治理逻辑。
