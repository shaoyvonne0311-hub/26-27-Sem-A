# GE1359 诊断卷 Diagnostic（60 min / 50 marks）

> [!warning] 闭卷限时
> 只允许 trig sheet（本次不给，自己记）。写完对 `Diagnostic Answer Key`，得分进 Excel `07 诊断 Diagnostic`。

## Q1 定义域与不等式 DR + INEQ（12 marks）

(a) Solve |2x − 5| < 7 and write the answer as an interval. **(4)**
(b) Find the domain of f(x) = √(x − 1) / (x² − 4). **(4)**
(c) Solve x² − 3x − 10 ≤ 0. **(4)**

## Q2 精确解与通解 EXACT + GENSOL（8 marks）

(a) Solve 2cos²x − cos x − 1 = 0 for 0 ≤ x ≤ 2π. **(4)**
(b) Find the general solution of tan(2x) = √3. **(4)**

## Q3 高斯消元 GAUSS（8 marks）

Solve
x + y + z = 6
2x − y + z = 3
x + 2y − z = 2

## Q4 AM–GM 与韦达 AMGM + VIETA（10 marks）

(a) For x > 0, find the minimum of x + 4/x and the x at which it occurs. **(5)**
(b) α, β are roots of x² − 5x + 6 = 0. Find α² + β² and α³ + β³. **(5)**

## Q5 归纳与递推 IND + RECUR（8 marks）

(a) Prove by induction that 1 + 3 + 5 + … + (2n − 1) = n². **(4)**
(b) Solve aₙ = 3aₙ₋₁ + 2, a₁ = 1. Give a closed form. **(4)**

## Q6 排列组合 COUNT（6 marks）

(a) Six people stand in a row. In how many ways with A and B **not** adjacent? **(3)**
(b) How many 3-digit even numbers can be formed from {1,2,3,4,5} without repetition? **(3)**
